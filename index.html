The problem

Family information is scattered. Actions get lost.

Messages. WhatsApp. Email. Screenshots. Notes. Calendar. Important details get buried, forgotten, or never turned into something someone can do.

Messages

“Can you pick up Maya at 16:00?” lives in a chat — and disappears.

WhatsApp

School updates sit next to stickers, photos, and yesterday’s plans.

Email

Appointments arrive as long threads nobody wants to file.

Screenshots

A photo of a flyer is not a reminder — unless someone makes it one.

Notes

Personal lists stay personal. The rest of the family never sees them.

Calendar

Events exist, but the tasks and reminders around them often do not.

The result: things slip. Someone was supposed to remember. Nobody did.

How it works

Share. Understand. Organize.

Three steps — without another system to remember. Family Brain works with information the way it already arrives.

01 — SHARE

Share

Share a message, screenshot, or note with Family Brain — from another app on Android, or from Home.

02 — UNDERSTAND

Understand

AI reads what the information means: what needs to happen, when, and who it is for.

03 — ORGANIZE

Organize

Family Brain turns it into the right task, reminder, event, or family note — ready for the household.

FAQ

Questions families usually ask.

What is Family Brain?

Family Brain is an AI-powered family organization app. It helps turn everyday messages, screenshots, and shared information into tasks, reminders, events, and family notes.

How does the AI work?

You share or send information to Family Brain. The AI reads what you shared and suggests the right task, reminder, or event. When details are unclear, the app asks you to confirm instead of guessing.

Can I use it with my family?

Yes. Family Brain includes a family workspace, personal space, assignments, calendar, activity, and notifications so everyone can see what matters.

Can I share information from other apps?

On Android, you can share text and images from other apps into Family Brain using the system share sheet. You can also paste or send information from inside the app.

Is Family Brain free?

The beta is free to try. Pricing can change after the testing period.

Where can I download the app?

Family Brain is not listed on Google Play or the App Store yet. When it is published, download buttons on this page will link to the official stores.
